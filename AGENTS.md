# Project instructions

Extractors can produce goods without input:
- aluminium
- wood
The output can be a limited amount in the extractor and can also send to a warehouse if road is available

Factories can produce goods if the input elements are fulfilled:
- tools
- car
- brick
- cloaths
- bread
The input for the factories must always be a warehouse. The output can be a limited amount in the factory and can also send to a warehouse if road is available


### Cities
A city can sell items only if a store is available and that store is selling the item
A city can include a company

### Companies
A company will provide a service such as Transport, Bus service, Health, Roads service, Car service
A company can buy and sell items to operate
